Loy Kandahar

One People, One History

Loy Kandahar is a platform dedicated to preserving the shared history, cultural roots, and true identity of Loy Kandahar, spanning from Kandahar down to Quetta. This website is owned and built by Atal Khan, an Afghan-Norwegian writer and entrepreneur born in Arghandab, Kandahar. He created this platform as a digital space to protect Kandaharian ancestral legacy and keep the shared cultural identity of the people alive across all areas..
